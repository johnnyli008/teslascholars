Most scholarships reward a transcript. This one funds a trajectory. Scholars receive capital, access, and a cohort of peers who treat "impossible" as a starting condition — not an answer.


Meet the alumni
01

The grant

$50,000 disbursed as $12,500 per academic year, for four years. Tuition, housing, parts, compute — spend it on what moves you forward. No repayment. No equity. No reporting theater.

$12,500 / YEAR · 4 YEARS
02

The mentorship

Every scholar is paired 1:1 with a practicing engineer, designer, or researcher. Monthly calls, factory walk-throughs, and honest feedback on the work — the kind you can't get from a syllabus.

1:1 MENTOR · MONTHLY CALLS
03

The network

160 alumni across Fremont, Berlin, Shanghai, and Austin — and a standing invitation to demo days, internships, and the annual build week. The cohort is the compounding asset.
